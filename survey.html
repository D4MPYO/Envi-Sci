DISASTER PREPAREDNESS SURVEY FOR STUDENTS

Survey Form

A. General Awareness

1. How well do you know about disasters?

2. Have you ever joined school or community activities about disaster safety?

3. Where do you usually hear news or warnings about typhoons, earthquakes, or other disasters?

B. Home Preparedness

4. Does your family have a "Go Bag" or emergency kit at home?

5. How often does your family check or change emergency supplies (food, water, first aid kit)?

6. Does your house have things that help protect it from strong winds or floods?

7. Have you or your family ever had your house damaged by a typhoon, earthquake, or other disasters?

C. Safety Knowledge

8. How sure are you that you know what to do during a typhoon, earthquake, or other disasters?

9. Do you know where the evacuation center in your area is?

10. Are you aware of any nearby safe places or higher ground you can go to during a disaster?

11. Have you and your family talked about where to meet or how to contact each other during a disaster?

12. Are you prepared to give basic first aid (like for small wounds) after a disaster?

D. Opinions and Concerns

13. Which dangers worry you the most? (You can check more than one)

14. What do you think is the hardest part about preparing for disasters?

15. Who do you think should teach and help the community prepare for disasters?

16. How important is it for neighbors to help each other during disasters?

E. Early Warning & Community Response

17. How good are the warning systems in your community?

18. Have you ever checked the Facebook page or website of your town's DRRMC for announcements?

19. If a big disaster happened today, how long do you think your family can manage without outside help?

F. Future Participation

20. Would you like to join disaster safety activities or training in the future?
